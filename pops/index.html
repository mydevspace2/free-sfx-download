About these pop sound effects

Pops are perfect for subtitles appearing word by word, emojis popping on screen, stickers and fast product shots. This category has a champagne cork, a double cork pop, a balloon burst, a single bubble-wrap pop, a suction cup pull and a short mouth-style pop.

Because pops are so short, they cut through music easily. Keep them a few decibels under your voice track, and vary between two or three different pops so a long subtitle sequence does not sound repetitive.

The pop sound effect is the most common sound in caption-heavy short videos: each word or emoji that appears gets a tiny pop. For that job, the mouth pop and the bubble-wrap pop are the cleanest because they are short and soft. The champagne cork and balloon pop are bigger and work better as one-off accents for celebrations, surprises and reveals. Every pop here starts instantly, so it lines up with the exact frame where the element appears.
