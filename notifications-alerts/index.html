About these notification & alert sound effects

Notification sounds draw attention to a message on screen, a new subscriber pop-up, a correct answer or a mistake. You get soft pings, a message-received tone, an "idea" ding, counter and hotel bells, a sparkling chime, success and confirmation tones, an error buzz and a question prompt.

Use the bells for "ding, correct!" moments in quizzes, the pings for chat and DM mock-ups, and the error buzz for fails and wrong answers. Short tones also make good app and game notifications.

All files are trimmed, faded and levelled (about -14 LUFS, peaks at -1 dBTP). See the License page for the original source of each sound.
