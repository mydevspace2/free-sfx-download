About these typing & paper sound effects

Typing and paper sounds suit text-reveal animations, storytelling channels, study videos and documentaries. This category has a typewriter key, space bar and the end-of-line bell, page turns from a paperback book and a paper crumple.

For a typewriter text effect, repeat the key sound for each letter with small timing changes, then finish the line with the bell.

All files are trimmed, faded and levelled (about -14 LUFS, peaks at -1 dBTP). See the License page for the original source of each sound.
