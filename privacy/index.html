Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-15

Free SFX Download (“we”, “the site”) is a static website. We do not ask you to create an account, and we do not collect your name, email address or any other personal details to download sounds.

Information collected automatically

Like every website, our hosting provider (GitHub Pages) receives technical data such as your IP address, browser type and the pages requested, in order to deliver the site and protect it from abuse. See the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Local storage

If you switch between light and dark mode, your choice is saved in your browser’s local storage. It never leaves your device.

Advertising

We may show ads from third-party advertising networks (such as Adsterra) to keep the site free. These partners may use cookies, web beacons or similar technologies to show ads and measure their performance, and may collect information such as your IP address, device and browser type. Their use of this data is governed by their own privacy policies. You can control cookies in your browser settings, and you can opt out of personalised advertising at youronlinechoices.com (EU) or optout.aboutads.info (US).

Analytics

We may use privacy-friendly analytics to count page views. If we do, this policy will be updated to name the provider.

Children

The site is not directed at children under 13 and we do not knowingly collect information from them.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA) you may have rights to access or delete data about you. Because we do not store personal data ourselves, requests about advertising data should go to the relevant ad partner. For anything else, see our Contact page.

Changes

We may update this policy. The date at the top shows the latest version.
