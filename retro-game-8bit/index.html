About these retro game & 8-bit sound effects

Retro game sounds are a favourite in gaming edits, explainer videos and meme content. This category combines chiptune-style effects from Juhani Junkala's CC0 "512 Sound Effects (8-bit style)" collection (jumps, power-ups, level-ups, a victory fanfare, damage hits, a game-over fall, errors, menu blips, a pause, a laser, an explosion, a low-health alarm, a portal warp and a falling bomb whistle) with announcer voice lines from Kenney's CC0 voiceover packs: "Game over", "You win", "You lose", "Level up", "New high score", "Mission completed", "Mission failed", "Fight!", "Round one", "Combo!", "Winner!", "Flawless victory" and "Go!".

Use the voice lines as punchy captions for challenges and comparisons, and the 8-bit sounds for pixel-art animations, progress bars and gamified tutorials.

A game over sound effect or a level up sound instantly turns a normal edit into a gamified one. Use 8-bit jumps and coins on progress bars and counters, the power-up on a glow-up, the game-over fall on a fail, and the announcer lines ("Fight!", "Round one", "Winner!") to frame comparisons and challenges. Everything here is CC0 and free for monetised videos and indie games.
