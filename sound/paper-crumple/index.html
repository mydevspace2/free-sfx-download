How to use this paper crumple sound in video editing

Download the MP3 for CapCut, VN, InShot and other phone editors, or the WAV for Premiere Pro, DaVinci Resolve and Final Cut Pro. Drop it on an audio track under the moment it belongs to; the sound starts on the first frame, so it lines up exactly where you place it. Keep it a few decibels below your voice track and trim or fade the end if you only need part of it.
