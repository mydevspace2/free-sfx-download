About these impact & hit sound effects

Impacts add weight to a cut, a title slam or a punchline. This category mixes close-miked punches, a door slam, a heavy metal gate, gong strikes, a whip crack and a set of designed metal, glass, plate and wood hits. The punches and whip crack are very fast transients, so they sound short and sharp rather than loud; layer one under a whoosh for a classic "swipe and hit" transition.

Use the gong hits and the metal gate slam for dramatic reveals and chapter titles, the punches for comedy and action edits, and the small wood and glass hits for UI-style motion graphics. All files are peak-safe at -1 dBTP so they will not clip your mix.

All files are trimmed, faded and levelled (about -14 LUFS, peaks at -1 dBTP). See the License page for the original source of each sound.
