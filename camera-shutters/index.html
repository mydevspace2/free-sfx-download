About these camera shutter sound effects

A camera shutter is the go-to sound for freeze frames, screenshot moments, photo slideshows and "snap" transitions. These are real recordings of a Nikon D70s DSLR, a Kodak Instamatic film camera, an old Kodak Brownie, a vintage flash camera with bulb, a burst-mode sequence and a smartphone camera.

Put the click exactly on the freeze-frame or flash frame. The burst-mode file suits paparazzi and fashion edits, while the film cameras give a warmer, retro feel for vlogs and travel videos.

Different cameras give different moods. A DSLR shutter sound effect is crisp and modern, the right choice for product photos, fashion edits and screenshot moments. The Kodak film cameras sound lighter and more mechanical, which suits nostalgic travel montages and family videos. The burst-mode file fires a rapid sequence for paparazzi and red-carpet gags, and the phone camera shutter matches content filmed or shown on a smartphone. Add a quick white flash frame on the click for the classic photo effect.
