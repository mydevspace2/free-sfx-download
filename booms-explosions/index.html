About these boom & explosion sound effects

Booms give a trailer moment, a logo reveal or a meme drop its punch. Here you get real recorded explosions (an agricultural bird-scaring cannon at close and far distance), thunder claps without rain, a firecracker bang, and designed crunchy explosions and deep low-frequency booms that work well on phone speakers and headphones alike.

For a cinematic hit, layer a short close explosion with a deep bass boom and cut the tail with a fade. Thunder files are longer and roll off naturally, which makes them good under slow-motion shots and dramatic text.

An explosion sound effect does not have to be long to feel big. The close and distant explosions here are real outdoor recordings, so they have natural air and echo, while the sub booms add the deep low end that phone speakers and earbuds turn into a felt thump. Use a short boom on a logo drop, a thunder clap on a dramatic line, and a firecracker bang for celebrations or surprise moments. Fade the tail early when the next scene starts with dialogue.
