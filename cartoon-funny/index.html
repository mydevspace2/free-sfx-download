About these cartoon & funny sound effects

Comedy edits live on well-timed cartoon sounds. Here you get harp boings, single and double air-horn honks and cartoon "twang" sounds of something sticking into a wall.

Place the sound right on the funny frame and keep it short: a boing on a jump, an air horn on a punchline, a twang when something lands. Do not overuse them; one or two per joke is enough.

All files are trimmed, faded and levelled (about -14 LUFS, peaks at -1 dBTP). See the License page for the original source of each sound.
