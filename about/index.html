About Free SFX Download

Free SFX Download is a small, fast library of free sound effects for video editors, short-form creators, streamers and indie game developers. We focus on the sounds editors actually use every day — whooshes, impacts, risers, pops, camera shutters, UI clicks, notifications, money sounds and glitches — and make each one ready to drop on a timeline.

What we do

  • Collect sound effects only from sources that release them as CC0 / public domain.
  • Choose realistic, punchy recordings and skip weak, noisy or unusable ones.
  • Trim leading silence, add short fades and level every file (about -14 LUFS, peaks at -1 dBTP).
  • Give each sound a clear, searchable name and offer MP3 and WAV with one-click download — no account, no email, no waiting.

Who it is for

YouTubers, TikTok and Reels editors, podcasters, teachers, students, app makers and game developers who need clean sounds they can use commercially without worrying about licences.
