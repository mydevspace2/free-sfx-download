About these glitch & sci-fi sound effects

Glitch and sci-fi sounds suit tech reviews, gaming edits, cyberpunk text effects and "error" moments. This set has computer data noise you can cut to any length, a digital static burst, laser blasts and shots, a retro laser and a UFO fly-by.
