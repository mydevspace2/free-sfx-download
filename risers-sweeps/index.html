About these riser & sweep sound effects

A riser builds tension before a reveal: the pitch or energy climbs and then stops exactly where the new shot or the beat drop lands. This small set includes a Shepard-tone riser (the endless rising illusion used in many film trailers), a spaceship pass that swells and fades, a force-field sweep, a phaser rise and two short digital power-up sweeps for game-style edits.

Line up the end of the riser with your cut, then add an impact or boom on the next frame for maximum effect. Shorter sweeps work well under text animations and app demos.

A riser sound effect works best when its end is locked to something: the cut, the beat drop, the product reveal or the punchline. Start the riser early enough that the build is felt, then stop it dead on the change and let an impact take over. The Shepard-tone riser suits film-style tension, the spaceship pass suits long sweeping transitions, and the short digital sweeps suit power-ups, level-ups and app feature reveals.
